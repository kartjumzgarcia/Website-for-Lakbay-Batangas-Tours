# Lakbay Batangas Tours

A single-page promotional website showcasing curated eco-tourism, cultural heritage, and adventure tour packages across the Batangas province in the Philippines.

> **Note:** This project is a front-end web development exercise. The contact details and inquiry forms use classroom placeholders and do not process real bookings.

---

## Features

* **Sticky Navigation Bar:** Quick jump-links to Home, About, Tour Packages, Why Choose Us, and Contact sections.
* **Destination Overview:** Introduction to Batangas highlights including Anilao, Mount Maculot, Taal Heritage Town, and Taal Lake.
* **Featured & Core Tour Packages:**
  * *Taal Heritage Tour* (Cultural & historical exploration)
  * *Package 1: The Anilao Deep Blue Escape* (Diving, snorkeling, and coastal retreat)
  * *Package 2: The Maculot Peak & Heritage Trail* (Hiking Mount Maculot and visiting Taal Town)
  * *Package 3: The Ultimate Nasugbu Beach & Island Hop* (Fortune Island and cove hopping)
* **Tour Duration Guide:** Structured HTML table comparing sample trip durations and inquiry links.
* **Traveler Preparation Lists:** Unordered checklist of essential items to bring and an ordered step-by-step guide on how to inquire.
* **Value Propositions ("Why Choose Us"):** Highlights curated itineraries, shuttle accessibility, certified local guides, and eco-first community tourism.
* **Sample Inquiry Section:** Front-end HTML form controls and placeholder contact information.

## Technologies Used

* **HTML5:** Semantic structure (`<header>`, `<nav>`, `<section>`, `<article>`, `<figure>`, `<table>`, and `<form>`).
* **CSS3:** External stylesheet (`css/style.css`) for layout, sticky navigation, and visual styling.

---

## Getting Started

To view this website locally:

1. Clone or download this repository to your computer.
2. Ensure `css/style.css` and `assets/images/taal-lake.jpg` are placed in their respective folders.
3. Double-click `index.html` to open it in any modern web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari).
